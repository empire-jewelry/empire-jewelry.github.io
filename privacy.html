Privacy

This page explains which data is processed when you visit empire-jewelry.github.io. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Empire Jewelry, Nabatieh, Lebanon. Contact: WhatsApp +961 70 894 976, Instagram @empirejewellery__.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the time and your browser type to GitHub’s servers. GitHub uses this data to deliver the website and keep it secure. Details: GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts and scripts from other servers

To show the fonts and animations, your browser loads files from these providers:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), Google LLC
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), for the animation library GSAP
  • unpkg (unpkg.com), for the smooth scrolling library Lenis

For this, your IP address reaches these providers. We do not send them any other data.

Your photo as a pendant (face detection)

When you upload a photo in “Your photo as a pendant”, the face is found and the background is removed directly in your browser. Your photo is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

For the face detection, your browser loads program files from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and model files from Google (storage.googleapis.com). This only happens after you choose a photo. As with any download, your IP address reaches these servers. The photo itself is not sent.

Your photo only reaches us if you send it yourself, for example on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp and Instagram

The “Send on WhatsApp” buttons open WhatsApp with a prepared message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Your message then goes to WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) and to us. The message can contain a link to your design; it only holds the options you chose. The picture of your design is copied to the clipboard on your device, and you decide whether to paste it.

Links to Instagram open Instagram (Meta Platforms). Their privacy rules apply there.

Cookies and storage

We do not use cookies, statistics or advertising trackers. If you turn on the music, your browser remembers this setting on your device (local storage, key “ej-music”). Nothing of this is sent to us.

What we do with your messages

We use the messages and photos you send us only to answer you, make your piece and arrange the delivery. We do not sell them or share them with others.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time which of your data we have, and ask us to correct or delete it. Just write to us on WhatsApp.
